# Research: Competitive

Map a competitive landscape on substance, not marketing copy.

## How to execute

- Identify the real players, not just the loudest marketing presence.
- Read changelogs, docs, and pricing pages directly rather than relying on third-party comparison posts, which are often stale or biased.
- Separate what a product *claims* from what it *actually does* — check GitHub issues or reviews for the gap between the two when it matters.
- Note what's genuinely differentiated vs. table-stakes across the set.

## Output

A structured comparison (use `compare-table` for the output artifact) with sourced, dated specifics — not adjectives.
