# Research: YouTube

Extract the actual argument from talks and interviews, not just the title.

## How to execute

- Pull the transcript rather than relying on the title/description, which often oversell or mischaracterize the content.
- Extract the core argument and the specific claims/numbers made, with approximate timestamps for anything you'd want to verify by watching.
- Distinguish the speaker's own experience/data from their speculation or opinion.

## Output

The argument in prose, cited to the video + timestamp, not a vague "a talk said X."
