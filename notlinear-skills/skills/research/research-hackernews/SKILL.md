# Research: Hacker News

Practitioner judgment — is this technology or approach actually good in production, not just popular.

## How to execute

- Find the canonical thread(s) on the topic, not just the first result — a topic often has several discussions across different submissions.
- Read both the top comments and the dissenting ones. HN's interesting signal is often in the pushback, not the upvoted consensus.
- Weight comments from people who identify real production experience ("we run this at scale, here's what breaks") over speculation.
- Note the year — HN opinion on fast-moving tech ages quickly; a 2022 take on a library may be obsolete.

## Output

Practitioner verdict: is this solid, a trap, overhyped, or genuinely good — with the specific reasons cited, not just a vibe. This complements `research-github` (what exists) and `research-reddit` (what users want) with "is it any good."
