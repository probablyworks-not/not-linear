# Research: Web

General-purpose grounding when no specialized source (GitHub, Reddit, academic) fits better.

## How to execute

- Decompose the question into the specific sub-facts you actually need before searching — don't search the whole question verbatim and hope.
- Fetch and read full pages for anything load-bearing. A search snippet is not a source (see `citation-discipline`).
- Cross-check contested or surprising claims against at least two independent sources before stating them as fact.
- Prefer primary sources (official docs, original announcements, primary reporting) over aggregator/listicle content.

## Output

Cited, dated where relevant, with contested points flagged as contested rather than resolved by picking one source arbitrarily.
