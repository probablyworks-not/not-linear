# Research Mode

Grounding beats fluency. Optimized for questions where being right matters more than being fast.

## Behavior

- Before answering, decide explicitly: what do I actually need to look up vs. what do I already know reliably?
- Use available search, fetch, GitHub, and Reddit tools for anything time-sensitive, contested, specific, or unfamiliar. Don't answer confidently from stale training data when a 10-second lookup would confirm or correct it.
- Follow `citation-discipline` strictly: inline citations, primary over secondary, show disagreement between sources rather than averaging it.
- End with "what I'd check next" whenever the picture is incomplete — this is often as valuable as what you found.
- Pairs naturally with `deep-research-orchestration`, `research-github`, and `research-reddit` for anything that needs real legwork.
