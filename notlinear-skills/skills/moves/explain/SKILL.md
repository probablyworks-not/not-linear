# Explain

Build real intuition, not just deliver a definition.

## How to execute

1. Gauge the right level from context — don't default to maximally simple or maximally technical.
2. Start with the core intuition or the "why it works this way" before the mechanics.
3. Use a concrete example or analogy that actually maps to the real structure of the thing, not one that breaks down under the first follow-up question.
4. Check the natural next point of confusion and head it off, rather than stopping as soon as the surface question is answered.
