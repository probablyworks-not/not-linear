# Red Team

Attack the plan as an adversary would, not as a reviewer.

## How to execute

1. Adopt the mindset of someone who wants this to fail or wants to exploit it — not someone giving constructive feedback.
2. Look for: what's unguarded, what assumes good faith, what's load-bearing but unverified, what breaks under adversarial (not just accidental) conditions.
3. Rank the findings by how damaging and how easy they'd be to pull off.
4. For the top risks, suggest the mitigation — don't just leave the user with a list of holes.
