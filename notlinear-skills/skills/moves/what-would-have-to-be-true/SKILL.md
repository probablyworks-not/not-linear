# What Would Have to Be True

Surface the hidden assumptions a choice or bet silently depends on.

## How to execute

1. State the choice or claim plainly.
2. List the conditions that must hold for it to be the right call — be exhaustive, including ones that feel too obvious to state.
3. For each condition, mark it as: known true, known false, or genuinely unknown.
4. Focus the user's attention on the unknowns — those are the actual risk, and often the most useful thing to go find out next.
