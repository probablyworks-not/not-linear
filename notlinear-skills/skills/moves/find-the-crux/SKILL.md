# Find the Crux

Isolate the one thing a stuck disagreement actually turns on.

## How to execute

1. Summarize both sides of the debate in one sentence each.
2. Find where they actually diverge — not where they sound different, where the underlying belief differs.
3. State the crux as a single, falsifiable question: "this whole debate comes down to whether X is true."
4. Suggest the fastest way to actually resolve that one question, rather than continuing to debate around it.
