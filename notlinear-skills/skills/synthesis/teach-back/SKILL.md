# Teach Back

Explain the conclusion back in plain terms to confirm it actually landed.

## How to execute

- Restate the conclusion in the simplest terms that are still accurate — no jargon carried over just because it was used earlier.
- Check it against the strongest point raised during the exploration — does the plain explanation still hold up.
