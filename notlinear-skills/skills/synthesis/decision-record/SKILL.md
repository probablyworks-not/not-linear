# Decision Record

Lightweight, durable record of a decision.

## Structure

- **Decision** — what was decided, stated plainly.
- **Context** — why this needed deciding now.
- **Alternatives rejected** — and the specific reason each lost.
- **Consequences** — what this commits to, and what it forecloses.
