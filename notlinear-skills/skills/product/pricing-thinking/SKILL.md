# Pricing Thinking

Reason through pricing structure, not just a number.

## How to execute

- Identify the value metric — what should price scale with, from the customer's perspective of value received.
- Reason about willingness-to-pay by segment, not a single blended guess.
- Consider tier structure only after the value metric is settled, not before.
