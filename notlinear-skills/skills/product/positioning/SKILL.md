# Positioning

Define differentiation concretely: who, against what, why it wins.

## How to execute

- Name the specific audience this is for — not everyone.
- Name what it's positioned against (the real alternative, which may be "doing nothing" or a manual process, not just named competitors).
- State why it wins for that audience specifically, in terms that audience would recognize as true.
