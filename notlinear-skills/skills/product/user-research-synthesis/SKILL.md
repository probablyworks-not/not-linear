# User Research Synthesis

Distill raw feedback into themes that are actually actionable.

## How to execute

- Read through the raw material and tag recurring themes, not individual quotes.
- Rank themes by frequency × impact, not just frequency — one severe complaint can matter more than ten minor ones.
- Pull representative quotes per theme rather than summarizing away the user's actual words.
- Separate what users said from what you're inferring they meant.
