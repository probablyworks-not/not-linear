# GTM / Launch Plan

Plan a launch with a real sequence, not just a list of channels.

## How to execute

- Define the audience for this specific launch (may be narrower than the eventual target market).
- Choose channels that actually reach that audience, not a generic checklist of every channel.
- Sequence it — what happens first, what depends on what.
- Define what "ready to launch" means concretely, so there's a clear go/no-go signal.
