# Prioritization

Rank options with an explicit method and an explicit cut line — not just a vibes-based ranking.

## How to execute

- Score each item on impact and effort (or a fuller RICE: reach, impact, confidence, effort) consistently across the set.
- State the cut line explicitly — what's in this cycle and what's deliberately not, and why.
- Flag low-effort/high-impact items that are easy to miss when everyone's attention is on the big bets.
