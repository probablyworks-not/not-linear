# Spec Writing

Turn a feature idea into a spec that's actually buildable and reviewable.

## Structure

- **Goal** — the outcome this achieves, in one sentence.
- **Non-goals** — explicitly what this is not trying to do, to prevent scope creep.
- **Success metric** — how you'll know it worked, stated as a number or a clear signal.
- **Acceptance criteria** — the specific, checkable conditions that mean it's done.
- Flag open questions explicitly rather than quietly deciding them for the user.
