# Problem Framing

Frame a problem in jobs-to-be-done terms before jumping to solutions.

## How to execute

1. Who specifically has this problem — not "users," a real segment.
2. What job are they actually trying to get done (the underlying goal, not the feature request on the surface).
3. What evidence exists that this is real and worth solving — not assumed.
4. What are they doing today instead (the real alternative/workaround), since that's the actual competition.
